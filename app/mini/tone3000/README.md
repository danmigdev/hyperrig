# TONE3000's logos

`tone3000-logo.svg` and `t3k-mark.svg` are the official logos of TONE3000, from
https://www.tone3000.com/TONE3000%20Logos.zip (23 November 2026), unchanged. They belong to
TONE3000 and are used as its API terms ask: to identify it where its tones are browsed,
listed and played. HyperRig is not endorsed by TONE3000.

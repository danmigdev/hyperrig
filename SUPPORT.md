# Support

Bugs and wishes go to the [issue tracker](https://github.com/danmigdev/hyperrig/issues), in
English. In the editor, **Report an issue on GitHub** opens the form; nothing is sent by itself.

Say what happened and what you expected, the steps, the pedal, how it is connected, your Windows,
and the version of HyperRig. **Copy diagnostics** in the editor and the files in
`%APPDATA%\HyperRig\logs` help: look at them first and send only the part that matters.

Do not attach the copy of a pedal (`%APPDATA%\HyperRig\pedals`), presets you want to keep to
yourself, or captures and IRs of others. The copies hold what is only yours and your pedal's.

There are no donations and no paid support.

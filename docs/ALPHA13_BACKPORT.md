## Announced scope to assess later

- Bluetooth music playback for playing along with songs.
- USB audio, including a separate clean guitar track for later reamping.
- Ping-Pong, Dual, Reverse and Vintage delay models.
- Stereo branches and additional fuzz and chorus models.
- Increased effect capacity per preset.
- A desktop editor for Windows, macOS and Linux.
